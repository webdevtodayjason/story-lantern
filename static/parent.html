What the verdicts mean

allow The page passed the deterministic blocklist and the independent classifier, and was read to your child as written.

softened The first draft of this page failed the safety check. It was thrown away and written again, gentler. The rejected draft is in the events table below the story, verbatim.

blocked_fallback Two drafts failed the safety check. The lantern read a pre-written safe page instead and the story ended early. Your child heard a slightly short story; they did not see an error.

unverified_fallback Different thing, and it is not about what was written. No draft failed — the classifier could not be reached at all (the device was busy with something else, or a model was unloaded), so nothing was ever reviewed. The lantern read a pre-written safe page rather than an unchecked one, and the story ended early.

allow_unverified The blocklist passed but the classifier could not be reached, and this lantern is set to show the page anyway (LANTERN_ALLOW_UNVERIFIED=1). The page was shown unreviewed. Worth reading these ones yourself.

allow_unchecked The independent classifier is switched OFF on this lantern (--no-safety, or safety_classifier: false in config.json). The page passed the deterministic blocklist only. That is a much weaker check than a plain allow, which is why it does not get one.

decline The lantern would not build a story from what was asked, and said something kind instead. The exact request is logged.

A local classifier will have false negatives. This log is not a substitute for a parent in the house — it exists so you can catch what the classifier didn't. Anyone who tells you a local model is unconditionally safe for a five-year-old is selling something.
